How this works

Your payment is based on the loan amount (price minus down payment), the interest rate and the term. Property tax and insurance are added on top so you see the full monthly cost.

Extra payments go straight to the loan balance, so you pay less interest and finish sooner. Even a small amount each month can save thousands over the life of the loan.
